# Offer — AI Video Ad Creative for Nordic Skincare Brands

One page. This is what we sell, to whom, at what price, with what promise.

## ICP (ideal customer profile)

Independent Nordic skincare/beauty/haircare DTC brand:
- Small team (1–15 people), founder reachable directly
- Sells own products online (Shopify/webshop)
- Already active on Instagram/TikTok, running or wanting paid ads
- Current video creative: scarce, founder-filmed, or expensive slow UGC creators

**Not ICP:** conglomerate-owned lines, retailers/marketplaces, brands with in-house creative teams.

## The pain we solve

- Ad creative fatigue: a paid social ad dies in 1–3 weeks; brands need a constant stream of fresh variants
- Human UGC creators: 1,000–3,000 NOK per video, 1–2 week turnaround, briefing loops, inconsistent quality
- Testing requires volume: winning ads come from testing 5–10 hooks per product; nobody can afford that with human creators

## Unit economics (internal — never in client material)

- Marginal cost ≈ 75 Higgsfield credits/video + revision regenerations (~2× buffer) → effectively < 100 NOK/video
- Test Pack margin ≈ 85–90%. Retainer 10/mo margin similar. Capacity constraint = credits, not hours — top-up/plan upgrade pays for itself from first retainer.

## Delivery SLA

- Test Pack: 72h from product info received
- Retainer: weekly batch, 48h turnaround
- Revisions: 2 rounds included (regeneration is cheap — generosity here costs nothing and closes deals)

## Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| "AI video looks fake" | You've already seen the spec ad — you tell me. Formats are platform-native UGC styles; we test hooks, your ad account decides. |
| "Who owns the videos?" | You do. Full commercial usage rights, raw files delivered. |
| "Norwegian language?" | Yes — scripts and voiceover in Norwegian (or SE/DK/EN). Native-language hooks outperform English in Nordic feeds. |
| "We already work with creators" | Keep them. We're the volume layer: test 10 hooks cheap, give winners to your creators to scale. |
| "Compliance?" | Videos are stylized ad creative, never presented as real customer testimonials — compliant with markedsføringsloven. We flag claim-risk phrasing in scripts. |
