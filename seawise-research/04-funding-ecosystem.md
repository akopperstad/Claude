**The single most important structural fact for 2026:** the national budget roughly **halved Innovasjon Norge's start-up schemes**. IN now **prioritises technology companies** and requires the **co-financing for Oppstartstilskudd 2 to be committed (in principle on the account) before a grant is awarded** ([regjeringen.no budget article](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/endrer-og-strammer-inn-det-naringsrettede-virkemiddelapparatet/id3124184/) [S, page blocked; content via search snippet]; [PNO summary](https://www.pnoinnovation.com/no/finansiering/oppstartstilskudd/) [S]). As a result, a small private round (or founder loans or savings) is now a **precondition** for OT2. It is no longer something you raise after the grant.

### 1.2 SkatteFUNN (Forskningsrådet + Skatteetaten)

- **Rate:** **19 %** of approved R&D costs, for all company sizes ([Forskningsrådet call 2026](https://www.forskningsradet.no/utlysninger/2026/skattefunn-skattefradrag-for-forskning-og-utvikling-i-et-nyskapende-naringsliv/) [V]).
- **Cap:** **NOK 25 million** in cost base per company per year [V].
- **Own personnel:** hourly rate = **1.2 ‰ of annual salary, max NOK 700/h, max 1,850 h per person per year** ([Sticos](https://www.sticos.no/fagstoff/skattefunn-dette-er-de-nye-endringene) [S]; [Forskningsrådet prosjektregnskap](https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/godkjenning/prosjektregnskap) [S]). **Only salaried work counts.** Founders' unpaid "sweat" does not, so the founders must be on payroll ([Forskningsrådet – hvem](https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/hvem/) [V]).
- **Loss-making companies get the credit paid out in cash** through the tax settlement [V].
- **Deadline:** rolling. Applications submitted **by 1 September** are guaranteed a decision in the same income year [V]. **For 2026 this date has passed.** Seawise should apply now so the project is approved for **income year 2027**.
- **Software eligibility:** software development counts as R&D when it resolves real technical uncertainty with no known solution, done systematically. It does **not** count if it is ordinary upgrades or applying existing software to a new use ([Forskningsrådet vurdering](https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/vurdering/) [S]). For NAUTECH, **ordinary ERP/CRUD features will not qualify.** Candidates that could qualify include:
  - AI/ML for predictive maintenance from sparse or heterogeneous vessel data
  - automatic extraction or structuring of class, flag and PMS documentation
  - offline-first sync over VSAT/4G at sea with conflict resolution
  - fuel and emission estimation models
  
  Write the application around these, with explicit R&D challenges, and keep timesheets from day 1.
- **Obligations:** project accounts and timesheets (signed continuously), auditor attestation of project accounts, and a status report by 1 March.
- **RISK, pending rule change:** in April 2026 the government sent out for consultation (deadline 1 Aug 2026) a proposal that **deductions only apply to costs incurred after approval** and a group-wide 25 MNOK cap ([regjeringen press release](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/regjeringen-trapper-opp-kampen-mot-okonomisk-kriminalitet-skjerper-skattefunn-regelverket/id3158178/); [høring](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/hoyring-om-tiltak-mot-misbruk-av-skattefunn/id3158164/) [S]). **Consequence: apply early, before the costs are incurred.**
- **Estimated value:** 2 founders at a NOK 600k salary each gives a rate of NOK 700/h (cap). At 1,200 R&D h each (~1.7 MNOK) plus ~0.5 MNOK of external dev/cloud/AI costs, the cost base is ~2.2 MNOK, giving **~NOK 400k per year**. If salaries are lower, scale the figure down.

### 1.3 Forskningsrådet: Innovasjonsprosjekt i næringslivet (IPN)

- **IPN Energi og transport 2026** includes a **Maritime** theme that explicitly covers "digitalisation of the maritime sector", green shipping and safety at sea. This theme is the successor to the old MAROFF programme. Maritime projects can get up to **NOK 20 MNOK**. The call is on **rolling intake** until funds run out, with a total budget of ~NOK 586M ([call page](https://www.forskningsradet.no/utlysninger/2026/innovasjonsprosjekt-naringslivet-energi-og-transport/) [V]).
- **IPN Industri og tjenestenæringer 2026:** NOK 1–16M per project, continuous intake **closing 1 Oct 2026 13:00**, project length 12–48 months ([call page](https://www.forskningsradet.no/utlysninger/2026/innovasjonsprosjekt-naringslivet-industri-og-tjenestenaringer/) [V]). There is also a **Sjømat** IPN for fisheries and aquaculture ([call](https://www.forskningsradet.no/utlysninger/2026/innovasjonsprosjekt-naringslivet-sjomatnaringen/) [S]).
- **Support rate for small enterprises:** ≤50 % for industrial research and ≤45 % for experimental development. **At least one collaboration partner** (a Norwegian company) **or bought-in R&D** (e.g. NTNU Ålesund, SINTEF Ålesund/Nordvest, Møreforsking) is required. The project must not have started before you apply [V].
- **Fit: MEDIUM for 2027.** A 2-person company needs to show it can finance its 50–55 % share. The typical path is IPN of ~3–6 MNOK in 2027–28 with NTNU Ålesund (Department of Ocean Operations and Civil Engineering) plus 1–2 pilot shipowners as partners. Watch Forskningsrådet's new application system and the 2027 calls ([autumn 2026 call overview](https://www.forskningsradet.no/nyheter/2026/utlysninger-host-2026-nytt-soknadssystem/)).

### 1.4 Regional and local (Møre og Romsdal / Herøy)

- **hoppid.no avklaringsmidlar:** up to **NOK 30,000 per business idea**. The money is for clarifying market, profitability and organisation. It is funded from county regional development funds via the hoppid.no municipalities ([Stranda regelverk](https://stranda.kommune.no/tenester/naring-og-skatt/etablererstotte-hoppid-no/regelverk) [S]). **Book a Hoppid adviser in Herøy now.** The same meeting is also the **mandatory gateway** to the Herøy næringsfond.
- **Herøy kommunale næringsfond (Næringsfond 2):** covers *etableringstilskot* for development or establishment phases. It requires business **and residential address in Herøy** (both founders need to check this), prior **Hoppid advice**, and application via **regionalforvaltning.no**. It normally covers **up to 50 % of the cost basis** and, exceptionally, **up to 75 %** by political decision ([Herøy vedtekter/retningslinje](https://www.heroy.kommune.no/_f/p36/i0a7e5906-1c65-4a36-832d-e9b378c1162a/lokal-retningslinje-for-kommunalt-naringsfond-2023-sak_29_23-14032023.pdf); [application template](https://heroy-no.kommune.no/getfile.php/5280212.1888.skzqpsbw7qjala/Mal+s%C3%B8knad+til+N%C3%A6ringsfond+2.pdf) [S]). Maximum amount per grant [U]: typical municipal funds give NOK 50–200k.
- **Møre og Romsdal fylkeskommune:** regional development funds for 2026 total ~NOK 75.4M, used for business development, incubator programmes and similar ([Statsbudsjett 2026 M&R](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/statsbudsjett/2026/fylkesoversikten/statsbudsjettet-2026-more-og-romsdal/id3122338/) [S]). Direct company grants usually flow via IN or hoppid.
- **Regionalt forskingsfond (RFF) Møre og Romsdal: *kvalifiseringsprosjekt*.** In May 2026 the county awarded **NOK 600k per project**, e.g. Havila Kystruten "Datadrevet beslutningsstøtte for energieffektivisering" and SINTEF Nordvest ([county decision document](https://mrfylke.no/api/presentation/v2/nye-innsyn/filer/v-41c1c2cb__4d43__484e__936a__a638e2bdddf8-1082487_1_P!d-1100481015!kVe0QG?pid=1) [S]). **HIGH fit.** A data-driven maritime project with an R&D partner is exactly the profile the county funds. Watch for the **2027 call** (spring deadline likely) [U on dates].

### 1.7 Savings-bank foundations

- **Sparebankstiftelsen Sparebanken Møre** (est. Nov 2024) gives gifts to non-profit purposes in M&R. ## 3. Private capital

### 3.1 Market context (2025–26)
- According to an aggregator, Norwegian maritime tech raised ~NOK 8bn in 2025, +29 % year on year ([ellty](https://www.ellty.com/investors/oslo-maritime-tech-investors) [S/U, looks AI-generated, do not quote in a deck]).
- **The state's pre-seed and venture capital top-ups are frozen.** Investinor and Nysnø got NOK 0 of fresh capital in the 2025 and 2026 budgets. FIN describes a tight early-stage market ([FIN input to Stortinget, Oct 2025](https://www.abelia.no/contentassets/be4aa53f7bd64738b4c2f1efb7d4854f/fins-innspill-til-naringskomiteen---statsbudsjettet-2026.pdf) [V]).
- **Investinor pre-seed matching:** Investinor invests NOK 10–50M into private pre-seed funds of NOK 30–150M, whose managers typically write **NOK 1–6M tickets** ([Investinor](https://investinor.no/en/pre-seed-matching) [V]). These funds are Seawise's natural lead investors for a pre-seed round.

### 3.2 Typical round sizes and valuations (Norway, B2B SaaS)
- **Pre-seed:** NOK 1–5M. Angels NOK 0.2–1M each, pre-seed funds NOK 1–6M. Pre-money is **typically NOK 10–30M** for a 2-founder team with a live pilot and no ARR, giving **10–20 % dilution** [S/U: triangulated from Investinor ticket guidance, StartupLab NOK 2–4M for ~10–15 %, and US B2B SaaS benchmarks of $650–750k rounds at 8–12 % dilution ([metal.so](https://www.metal.so/collections/average-pre-seed-round-size-us-saas-startups-2025-data-benchmarks))].
- **Seed:** NOK 10–40M at NOK 40–120M pre-money, which requires recurring revenue, typically NOK 2–5M ARR, or strong pipeline and pilots. Comparable: **Ofiniti** (Oslo maritime fuel platform), $6.8M seed, March 2026 (DNV Ventures, Nysnø, Verb, IN) [S]; **VesselMan** (maritime maintenance SaaS), ~$1.1M total from Skagerak Maturo, Thomas Grieg, Mokster and IN, then acquired by Marcura in Jan 2025 ([Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/marcura-acquires-vesselman-from-skagerak-capital) [S]). VesselMan is a **highly relevant exit comparable** for NAUTECH.

## 5. Norwegian AS practicalities (2-person startup)

- **VAT (MVA):** registration is mandatory once taxable turnover exceeds **NOK 50,000 in a 12-month period**. **Pre-registration (forhåndsregistrering)** is possible before you reach the threshold if you have made significant investments, which lets you reclaim input VAT on development costs. B2B SaaS sold abroad is generally outside Norwegian VAT (reverse charge in the customer's country). Filing is bi-monthly. [Standard rule, confirm at skatteetaten.no]
- **Aksjonæravtale (shareholder agreement):** sign it **before** any external money comes in. Cover:
  - vesting and reverse vesting of founder shares, with good/bad-leaver clauses, even between brothers
  - drag- and tag-along rights
  - pre-emption rights
  - decision rules (board versus general meeting)
  - IP assignment from the founders to SEAWISE AS (critical for SkatteFUNN, IN and due diligence)
  - non-compete
  - deadlock resolution
- **Holding company structure:** each founder should own through a personal holding AS. Under the **exemption method (fritaksmetoden)**, only 3 % of dividends or gains is taxable (effectively ~0.66 %), which enables tax-deferred reinvestment and angel activity. The cost is ~NOK 30k share capital plus accounting per holding company. **Do this before any value-raising event** (the first round or a revenue milestone). A later restructuring via a tax-free share exchange is possible but costs more.
- **Employee options:** the special startup option scheme was **widened from 13 March 2025**. It now applies to companies **≤12 years old**, with **≤150 FTEs**, **≤NOK 80M in revenue** and **≤NOK 200M balance sheet** in the previous year. There is **no tax at grant or exercise**; tax falls due only when the shares are sold (capital gains tax) ([regjeringen](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/aktuelt/utvidingane-av-opsjonsskatteordninga-for-oppstarts-og-vekstselskap-trer-i-kraft-13.-mars-2025/id3091435/); [Skatteetaten guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/business-and-organisation/reporting-and-industries/third-part-data/bank-finans-og-forsikring/opsjoner-i-oppstartsselskap/om-ordningen-med-tildelte-opsjoner-i-sma-oppstartsselskaper/) [V]). There are reporting requirements to Skatteetaten. Set up a 10–15 % option pool at the pre-seed round.
- **Altinn and filing calendar:**
  - **a-melding** monthly once anyone is on payroll. Put the founders on salary to build a SkatteFUNN cost base.
  - **MVA-melding** every 2 months.
  - **Aksjonærregisteroppgave** by 31 Jan.
  - **Skattemelding** for AS by 31 May, including the SkatteFUNN form RF-1053.
  - **Årsregnskap** to Brønnøysund by 31 July.
  - SkatteFUNN status report by 1 March.
  - Register beneficial owners (RRH).
  - Pension (**OTP**) is mandatory once there are employees on salary.
- **Auditor:** small AS can opt out, but **SkatteFUNN project accounts need auditor attestation**, and investors and IN like audited accounts. Budget NOK 30–60k per year.
