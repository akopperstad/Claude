# LinkedIn outreach: final version (M1)

Merged from 5 panel reviews (`panel/reviews/outreach-*.md`): sales, MIT coach, brand, red team, superintendent.

**What all 5 agreed on:**
- The first line is a question, not an introduction.
- Remove "ingen salgspitch", because saying it makes the message read as a pitch.
- The product is mentioned only in the signature (honest, about 10%).
- One concrete question they can answer directly in the reply.
- Two fixed time slots.
- Something to give back: an anonymous summary of how other shipping companies handle it.

## Rules
- Only write things that are true. Fill in the "own experience" line with your own words, or delete it.
- If they reply to the question in the message itself, that is already data. Write it into the CRM.
- **Friendly-network bias (MIT coach):** your followers are friendlier than the market. Also mix in contacts you don't know, from `prospects-fishing.csv` and `prospects-other-segments.csv`.
- Don't contact anyone at Lerøy group companies or DeepOcean. Fishing waits until you have the lawyer's written advice on your duty of loyalty.
