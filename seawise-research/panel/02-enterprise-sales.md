## 0. The short version

You don't have a sales problem yet. You have a **focus and credibility problem**, and it would be made worse by chasing an ASA co-development partner.

A listed shipowner will not put a 2-person company with NOK 30k share capital, no security documentation and no offline mode into its fleet. It will do one of two things:
- take your meetings as free market intelligence, or
- run a 6–12-month "pilot" that eats your runway and ends in a procurement process you lose to DNV or AMOS.

The money and the references in the next 12 months are in **3–15-vessel family rederier on Sunnmøre and Nordvestlandet**. These owners:
- decide at the kitchen table
- know your family name and home port
- can drive to Fosnavåg to see you
- will tell ten other owners at the Fiskebåt Vest meeting if you're good.

Sell them **one wedge, not an ERP**. The wedge is maintenance, certificates and survey/audit readiness for fishing and offshore vessels, with free migration from PreMaster or Excel. Charge from day one.

### 1.3 Segments that are missing

1. **The ocean fishing fleet ("havfiskeflåten") beyond the big names.** Most ringnot (purse seiners), trawl and autoline vessels are owned by companies with 1–5 vessels. The list has fewer than 40 fishing names. Fiskeridirektoratet's vessel register has them all, with org numbers, as open data ([fiskeridir.no](https://www.fiskeridir.no/registre/fartoyregisteret/fartoy-og-eieropplysninger), [data.norge.no](https://data.norge.no/en/datasets/2058f4e4-7fce-4b3f-ad34-34dbf526297d)).
2. **Coastal fishing, 15–28 m.** Sjøfartsdirektoratet's simplified safety management rules (in force since 1 July 2017) require most commercial vessels outside the ISM Code to have a maintenance plan that follows manufacturer recommendations ([Sdir brochure](https://www.sdir.no:443/globalassets/brosjyrer/sikkerhetsstyring-pa-mindre-fartoy-2022.pdf)). Many owners in this group run it on paper today. That's a large, cheap-to-serve segment. Exact scope and thresholds are for memo `02-segmentation`.
3. **Mid-size offshore and specialist ship managers on Sunnmøre.** Examples: Sanco Shipping (Gjerdsvika, about 7 offshore vessels, [MagicPort](https://magicport.ai/owners-managers/norway/sanco-holding-as)), plus smaller Ulstein/Hareid/Herøy managers.
4. **Owners with newbuilds on order.** A newbuild needs a PMS built from scratch, so the incumbent has no data lock-in. Nergård Havfiske has a series of Vard-built trawlers ([Baird](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/fishing/catching/trawling/vard-to-build-second-trawler-in-series-for-nergard-havfiske)); Liegruppen took delivery of *Libas* ([Baird](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/fishing/fishing-boat-world-reviews/vessel-review-libas-hybrid-seiner-trawler-delivered-to-norways-liegruppen)). Yard order books are a lead source.
5. **Channels.** None are listed:
   - Fiskebåt and its regions
   - Kystrederiene
   - the yards (Vard, Ulstein, Myklebust, Cemre's Norwegian clients)
   - naval architects (Salt, Skipsteknisk)
   - marine electronics installers
   - approved companies that audit small vessels.

**Company row:**
- org no.

## 3. Sales cycle and DMU

### 3a. Large ASA (e.g. Havila Shipping, Eidesvik, DOF, Solstad, Lerøy Seafood Group)

| Role | Who | Power |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | CTO / VP Technical / COO; CFO for anything over about NOK 1M/yr | Signs |
| Champion | Technical superintendent, fleet manager, QHSE manager | Carries the business case |
| Users | Chief engineers, captains, office purchasers | Can kill adoption silently |
| **Veto: IT/security** | Group CIO or outsourced IT; the ISMS owner | Asks for ISO 27001/SOC 2 or a questionnaire, DPA, pen test, SSO, backup/restore, data residency, offline mode, financial stability (they **will** look at your NOK 30k share capital on Proff) |
| **Veto: loyal to the incumbent** | The technical manager who implemented AMOS or ShipManager 8 years ago | "Our historical data lives there" |
| Influencer | Class society (for vessels on a PMS-based continuous machinery survey arrangement, a change of system triggers a new implementation survey *(verify the process with DNV)*), insurer, flag, external auditor | Can delay |
| Purchasing | Procurement / legal | Contract terms, liability cap, escrow |

- **Cycle:** 9–18 months from first meeting to fleet rollout. That's typically:
  - 2–4 months of discovery
  - 1–3 months of security review
  - a 3–6-month pilot on 1–2 vessels
  - procurement
  - fleet rollout vessel by vessel.
- **Budget:** set Sep–Nov for the next calendar year. If you're not in the 2027 budget by November 2026, **the realistic first fleet revenue is 2028**. Only small pilots come out of an "innovation" or OPEX line.
- **Contracts:** incumbent contracts are typically 3–5 years, often enterprise-wide *(verify against `01-competitors.md`)*.
- **Switching barriers:**
  - remaining contract term
  - migrating historical job and SFI libraries and running hours
  - class re-approval
  - crew retraining over rotations (about 6 months for everyone to have seen the new system)
  - integrations (ERP/purchasing, crewing, payroll).

### 3b. Family rederi with 3–15 vessels (e.g. Sanco, Remøy, Ervik, pelagic owners)

| Role | Who | Power |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | Reder / daglig leder, who is often also the main shareholder, sometimes the skipper | Signs, often on a handshake followed by a 2-page agreement |
| Champion | Teknisk sjef / driftssjef (in many 1–3-vessel companies, the chief engineer) | Does the evaluation |
| Veto | The chief engineer who "has the system in his head"; the external IT supplier or accountant; the approved company that audits them | Soft veto |
| Influencer | Peer owners, the Fiskebåt network, the yard, the family board | Strong: word of mouth |

- **Cycle:** 1–4 months if the pain is real and trust exists. Usually two meetings plus one visit on board.
- **Timing:**
  - Avoid peak season. For pelagic that's the autumn mackerel/herring season and the winter herring/capelin season; for whitefish trawl it's the Jan–Apr skrei (winter cod) season.
  - Onboarding works best when vessels are at the quay or in the yard.
  - Budgets are flexible: a NOK 50–150k/yr decision doesn't need a budget line.
- **Contracts:** 1–3 years, often rolling annual terms with small vendors.
- **Switching barriers:** mostly effort, and fear of an audit finding during the changeover.
  - Remove the effort: do the migration yourselves.
  - Remove the fear: run in parallel until the next survey or audit passes.

**Implication:** a family rederi is 4–6× faster, and you can win it with what you have. An ASA needs things you don't have yet: security documentation, offline mode, SLA, financial strength. Do family rederier first and build the ASA prerequisites in parallel.

### 4.2 Legal note on cold email in Norway

Markedsføringsloven § 15 bans unsolicited electronic marketing to *natural persons* without prior consent. Forbrukertilsynet's guidance says that if you're unsure whether an address belongs to a person, you must get consent first ([Forbrukertilsynet guidance](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Veiledning-om-markedsføring-via-epost-sms-ol.pdf); [NEF summary](https://nef.no/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Orienteringsbrev-om-markedsføring-via-SMS-e-post-og-telefon-.pdf)). A named work address (ola@rederi.no) is risky for mass mailings.

**Practical rule:**
1. **Phone first.** B2B calls are fine.
2. LinkedIn next.
3. Email only as a 1:1, individually written **request for an interview** (not an offer), to generic addresses (post@), or after verbal consent on the phone ("Kan jeg sende deg en e-post?").
4. No automated sequences to personal work addresses.

Have a lawyer confirm this.

## 5. Selling credibly as 2 people with an MVP against DNV, AMOS and PreMaster

**Your real advantages:**
- **Proximity.** You can be at the quay in 30 minutes.
- **The founders are users.** You are marine engineers, not salespeople.
- **Speed.** Fixes in days, not release cycles.
- **Price.**

**Your real liabilities:** a buyer will ask "Will you exist in 2 years?", "Does it work offline?" and "Where is our data?"

**Risk reversal, in priority order:**

1. **Parallel run, no rip-and-replace.** Keep the incumbent until the next audit or class survey passes on Nautech. This neutralises the "loyal technical manager".
2. **Data-export guarantee, written into the contract.** A full export at any time, in open formats (CSV/JSON, SFI structure preserved, attachments included), within 5 working days, at no charge, including at termination. Demonstrate it in the pilot.
3. **Free migration in** from PreMaster, Excel or paper, and in the same contract, **free migration out**.
4. **Short commitment.** 12 months, then rolling with 3 months' notice. The incumbents want 3–5 years; your short term is a weapon.
5. **Refundable pilot fee** tied to *your* obligations (section 4.4).
6. **A security package before the first ASA meeting:**
   - a 2-page security note (hosting region, encryption, backups/restore test, MFA, role-based access)
   - a DPA (databehandleravtale; crew data is personal data under GDPR)
   - an external pen test (budget about NOK 50–100k; a good use of Innovation Norway money)
   - fixes for the public-bundle and data-model exposure
   - disclosure of which AI sub-processors see customer data (the Lovable AI gateway will be questioned).
   - Plan for ISO 27001 later. Don't claim it.
7. **Offline mode.** For fishing and offshore, this is a **hard gate**, not a feature. Don't sign a pilot until offline sync works on a real trip.
8. **SLA.** 99.5% monthly uptime, a response within 4 working hours (1 hour for critical issues at sea), service credits of 10% of the monthly fee per breach. Small money, big signal.
9. **Continuity.**
   - Source-code escrow is overkill for NOK 100k/yr deals. Offer it for contracts over about NOK 500k/yr (a Norwegian law firm or escrow agent can hold it).
   - Until then, a written **continuity clause:** if Seawise stops operating, the customer gets a perpetual licence and the latest export.
   - Raise share capital, or show funding (Innovation Norway, hoppid). A NOK 30k balance sheet on Proff is the first thing a CFO sees.
10. **Borrowed credibility.** An advisory board of one respected retired teknisk sjef or reder from Sunnmøre (paid in options), and a quote from Fiskebåt or a yard. That's worth more than any feature.

**Pricing posture:** transparent, per vessel, one wedge. No per-module menu of 12 items. Complexity is a reason not to buy.
