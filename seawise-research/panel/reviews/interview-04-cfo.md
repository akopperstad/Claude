# Review: `08-telefonintervju-15min.md`, from the VC/CFO seat

*Date: 30 Sept 2026. Reviewer: panel 04 (VC partner + CFO).*

## Verdict

**A good Mom Test guide, but a weak data-collection instrument.**

The script asks the right *kinds* of questions: about the past, with concrete examples and no pitching. The problem comes when we try to use the answers:
- For **MIT steps 4, 16, 17 and 19**, the Innovation Norway OT1/OT2 applications and a pre-seed deck, we need the **same numbers from every call, in a form we can add up**.
- Today the script produces good stories and a lot of "vet ikke" (don't know) on cost.

After 20 calls, the questions investors and Innovation Norway will ask have no answer yet:
- What is the median spend per vessel?
- How many vessels in the pipeline have a renewal in the next 12 months?
- How many hours of admin does the product save?
- How many prospects agreed to a next step with the person who pays?

## Top 5 changes

### 1. Do the fleet research before the call, not on it
Don't spend call minutes on facts that are public. Before each call, fill in from:
- **Fiskeridirektoratet's vessel register**: number of vessels, length, build year
- **Brreg/Proff**: revenue
- **the DNV vessel register**: class society
- **PreMaster/Sirkel reference lists**: a guess at the current system.

On the call, only confirm the scope:
> «Jeg ser dere har [X] fartøy. Er det alle båtene som ville vært aktuelle for et vedlikeholdssystem, eller bare noen av dem?»

### 2. Ask about cost as **structure, then bracket**, never as an exact number
The current follow-up, «Omtrent hva koster det per fartøy i året…», invites a "vet ikke" or discomfort. Structure is not sensitive, and a bracket is easy to answer.

Rewrite of Q1's follow-up:
> «Hvordan er det priset: per båt, per bruker, eller en fast sum for hele rederiet? Er det en årsavgift i tillegg?»
> «Bare grovt, ikke eksakt: er det under 50 000 per båt i året, mellom 50 og 150 000, eller over det?»
> Hvis de nøler: «Helt greit. Vet du om det ligger på båtens budsjett eller på kontoret?»

Why this works:
- The bracket boundaries match our pricing evidence (TM Master ≈ 100k, PreMaster ≈ 200k per vessel per year).
- The answers aggregate straight into a spend distribution for step 16 and the TAM.
- Do **not** mention the prices of other shipowners you have talked to. That leaks confidential data and anchors the answer.

### 3. Add renewal date and notice period: the number that sets the sales cycle
With a yearly fee, switching only happens at renewal. Without that date we cannot forecast.

Add it to Q4:
> «Hvor bundet er dere til dagens løsning: er det en årlig avtale? Omtrent når fornyes den, og er det oppsigelsestid?»

If they don't know:
> «Hvem hos dere ville visst det?»

That answer also tells you who holds the contract, which is a member of the decision-making unit (DMU).

### 4. Put a number on the pain: hours per week, and effort of the last implementation
Q3's story is good. Add one number after it:
> «Omtrent hvor mange timer i uka går med til vedlikeholds- og sertifikatadministrasjon, for maskinsjefen om bord og for teknisk på land?»

And add to Q4 or Q5, for COCA and the onboarding fee:
> «Da dere innførte dagens system: hvor lang tid tok det før det var i full drift, og hadde dere hjelp utenfra?»

Add to Q5, for the approval path:
> «Kan teknisk avdeling bestemme noe sånt selv, eller må det opp i ledergruppen eller styret?»

### 5. Close with commitments, and get them in writing
The current close («Kan jeg komme tilbake om noen uker…») produces a polite yes that is worth nothing as evidence. Replace it with a commitment ladder:
> «Takk, dette var nyttig. Kan jeg sende deg en kort oppsummering på e-post av det jeg hørte, så du kan rette opp hvis jeg har misforstått?»

*(Their reply confirms the pain in writing. That is evidence for Innovation Norway and investors, and it is harmless for them.)*

> «Vi skal teste en betalt pilot med et par rederier i vinter. Vil du at jeg tar kontakt når den er klar, og hvem hos dere burde i så fall være med i den samtalen?»

*(This is not selling. It asks permission and gets the name of the budget owner.)*

Keep «Hvem andre bør jeg snakke med…», and ask for a **name**, not a company.

Fleet facts come off the call (change 1). That frees about 1 minute.

## New fields for the note template
Keep these as **one row per call in the CRM/CSV** so they can be aggregated for the IN application and the investor deck:

| Field | Format |
|---|---|
| Vessels total / in scope | number / number |
| Incumbent system | PreMaster / TM Master / AMOS / Star / Excel / paper / other |
| Pricing structure | per vessel / per user / fleet / unknown; yearly fee yes/no |
| Spend per vessel per year | <50k / 50–150k / >150k / unknown |
| Budget holder | vessel / office |
| Renewal month; notice period | month; months |
| Admin hours per week (on board / ashore) | number / number |
| Last implementation: duration, external help | months; yes/no |
| Decision: technical dept alone / management / board; budget month | code; month |
| Class-approved maintenance | yes / no / unknown |
| Pain intensity (interviewer judgment) | 1–5 |
| **Commitment level** | 0 = none · 1 = summary email accepted · 2 = named budget owner / intro given · 3 = pilot conversation booked · 4 = LOI/paid pilot |

**Target after the 20 calls in M1** (numbers we can show Innovation Norway and investors):
- at least 12 spend brackets and 12 renewal months collected
- at least 8 prospects at commitment level ≥2
- at least 3 at level ≥3.
