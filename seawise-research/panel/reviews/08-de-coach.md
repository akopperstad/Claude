# Review 08: DE coach audit of PLAN.md against MIT DE, *Startup Tactics* and ÅKP/FølgOpp

**Reviewer:** Disciplined Entrepreneurship coach / operations (panel 08).
**Date:** 30 Sep 2026.
**Inputs:** `PLAN.md` and `20-synthesis-and-verdict.md`, including the founder answers. New facts this round:
- The founders' trawlers run class-approved PMS.
- PreMaster costs about NOK 16k per vessel per month plus a yearly fee; TM Master about NOK 100k per vessel per year.
- Arne owns sales.
- The kill rule is accepted.

## Verdict: **AGREE WITH CHANGES**

The plan finally does the three things DE cares about most:
- one beachhead;
- selling before building;
- paid commitments as the proof point.

It is much better than `10-roadmap.md`. But it has five method defects that will bite within 60 days:

1. **The beachhead is now self-contradictory.** The synthesis (§4) defines the beachhead as fishing vessels *without* a class-approved PMS. The founders then answered that the trawlers they know *do* run class-approved PMS. So the founders' home segment largely falls *outside* the beachhead, and the TAM (step 4) and persona (step 5) are unverified. PLAN.md still says only "Norwegian fishing fleet".
2. **Steps 6, 8, 10, 11, 13 (partly), 18, 20 and 21 have no milestone.** Step 8 (value) sits in M8 (Apr 2027), *after* the pilots it should justify. The pilot "written success criteria" can't be written without a step 8 as-is baseline.
3. **Step 22 (M4, the MVBP) is built before steps 8, 20 and 21.** The MVBP is scoped in week 1, before Gate 1. The PreMaster importer is committed before we know how many beachhead prospects use PreMaster rather than paper or Excel. This is the confirmation-bias trap of an existing MVP.
4. **The kill rule is under-specified.** If "qualified meeting" counts discovery interviews, it can fire around week 5, before any offer has been made (offers start in weeks 6–8).
5. **The operating system is missing from the single source of truth.** The build cap, capacity cap, decision log, gate criteria and scoreboard targets live only in the synthesis or memos, not in PLAN.md. So they won't be run.
