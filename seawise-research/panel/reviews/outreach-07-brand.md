# Outreach message: brand/marketing seat

## Message (429 characters)

```
Hei [navn]! Hvor mange system bruker dere for å holde styr på vedlikehold, sertifikat og klasse på [brønnbåtene] i dag?

Jeg er maskinist fra Herøy og intervjuer 20 rederier om dette i høst. Broren min og jeg bygger programvare for det, men nå vil jeg bare lære.

Har du 20 min på telefon neste uke? Du får en anonym oppsummering av hvordan de andre rederiene løser det, og hva det koster dem i tid.

Arne Kopperstad
```

**[brønnbåtene]** changes by segment: *brønnbåtene* (wellboat), *offshorefartøyene* (offshore), *skipene* (short-sea).

## Rationale
The preview line is a question the reader can answer without thinking, not a self-introduction.

"Ingen salgspitch" is gone, because saying it makes the message sound like one. The disclosure that we build software stays in, briefly, for honesty.

What they get in return is concrete and peer-driven: how the other rederier solve it, and what it costs them in time.

The tone is direct and plain, like one engine-room person writing to another.

## Rules
- **Send it only after the 48-hour website fix.** The reader will click through to the site.
- **Send it personally from Arne's profile.** No automation tools.
- **Follow up once, after 5 days,** with one line: "Skjønner at det er travelt – er det en annen på teknisk jeg heller bør snakke med?" This also maps the decision-making unit (DMU).
- **Keep the promise.** Actually send the anonymised summary to everyone. That is the second touchpoint, and it will get you your pilot meetings.
